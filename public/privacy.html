Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Peachy is a small, independent tool that updates YouTube video titles on a creator's behalf. This page explains, in plain terms, what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Google account info: your name and email address, from Google sign-in.
  • YouTube data: the titles, descriptions, and public transcripts of the specific videos you choose to include in a job — nothing from videos you don't select.
  • A YouTube access token: encrypted at rest, used solely to read those videos' current metadata and publish new titles on the schedule you set up.
  • Payment record: our payment processor (Polar) tells us a payment succeeded and its amount. We never see or store your card details.
  • Your own optional notes: any short summary you type in about a video, to help the title generator understand it better.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't access videos, playlists, or channel data outside the specific job you set up.
  • We don't post, delete, or modify anything on your channel other than the title field of the videos you selected.

Who we share it with

To do its job, Peachy sends the minimum necessary data to these processors:

  • Google / YouTube Data API — to read and update your video titles.
  • Groq, and Google Gemini as a fallback — receive the video's title, description, and a transcript excerpt to generate a new title suggestion. They do not receive your name, email, or account info.
  • Resend — sends your weekly update email; receives your email address and the email content.
  • Polar — handles checkout and payment; receives your email address for the receipt.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the app and its database (Workers + D1).

We don't share data with anyone beyond what's needed to run these specific parts of the service.

How long we keep it

Account and job data is kept while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time (see Contact, below) — this also revokes our access token immediately.

Revoking access

You can revoke Peachy's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account settings, under Security → Third-party apps with account access, independently of anything on our end.

Cookies

Peachy sets one cookie: a signed session cookie so you stay logged in. It contains no tracking data, and we don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

Children

Peachy is not directed at, and is not knowingly used by, children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@pea.dpdns.org
