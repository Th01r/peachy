What Peachy does

You select 5 videos from your own YouTube channel, a title style, and a psychological angle. In exchange for a one-time payment, Peachy generates and publishes a new title for each of those 5 videos once a week for 12 weeks, based on the video's content and current search trends, and emails you a summary after each run with a one-click link to revert any single title back to what it was.

Eligibility

You must own or have the legal authority to manage the YouTube channel and videos you submit, and be old enough to form a binding contract in your jurisdiction.

Payment and cancellation

  • Each job is a single, one-time payment — not a subscription, and no card is kept on file for recurring billing.
  • Payments are non-refundable. You can cancel a job at any time from your account page; cancelling stops future weekly runs but does not refund runs already completed.
  • A job automatically ends after its 12th weekly run.

About the Google "unverified app" warning

Peachy is a small tool serving a limited number of creators and has not completed Google's full app-verification review. When you connect your channel, Google shows an "unverified app" warning — this is expected. The only permission we request is the ability to read and update your video titles; we cannot access anything else on your account through this connection.

Capacity limits

To stay within API and infrastructure limits, Peachy caps how many jobs can run at once. If we're at capacity, new jobs are temporarily unavailable until a slot frees up — this doesn't affect any job already in progress.

Acceptable use

You won't use Peachy on videos you don't have the rights to manage, or to generate titles for content that's illegal, that infringes someone else's rights, or that violates YouTube's own policies. We may cancel a job without refund if we reasonably believe it's being used this way.

No performance guarantee

Peachy generates title suggestions using automated tools and current trend data as a best-effort process. We don't guarantee any specific change in views, clicks, or channel performance, and a generated title may occasionally be imperfect — that's exactly why every change is revertible.
